    How this was measured

    Pairs are BMTC stops 12–70 metres apart that do not share a parent station. For every pair with at least 400 scheduled arrivals a day, Amazon Location calculated the pedestrian route from one side to the other. A gap means that walk is at least three times the straight-line distance and more than 150 metres longer.

    Counts are scheduled bus arrivals, not passengers. A long walk can also mean the walking map is missing a crossing that exists on the ground.
